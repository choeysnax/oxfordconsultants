Notice of Annual General Meeting

Notice is hereby given that the 46th Annual General Meeting of NSIA INSURANCE COMPANY LIMITED (the "Company") will be held on Tuesday, 6th May, 2025 at 0900h GMT by Zoom to transact the following business:

Date: Tuesday, 6th May 2025

Time: 09:00 AM GMT

Venue: Virtual Meeting via Zoom

Link: Click here to register. The Zoom link will be sent to your email after registration

Agenda

  1. To waive the notice period required for this meeting;
  2. To receive and consider the Accounts and Reports of the directors and auditors for the year ended 31st December, 2023;
  3. To receive the directors' statement on dividend for the year ended 31st December, 2023;
  4. To authorize the directors to fix the remuneration of the auditors;
  5. To ratify the appointment of Mrs. Monica Amissah as a director of the company, subject to the approval of the National Insurance Commission.
  6. To ratify the remuneration of the non-executive directors.
  7. To approve the payment of end-of-service benefits to the non-executive directors.
  8. To transact any other business appropriate to be dealt with at an Annual General Meeting.

Note: A member of the company entitled to attend and vote is entitled to appoint a proxy to attend and vote in his/her stead. A proxy need not be a shareholder. A proxy form is available in the registration link that has been provided. It must be completed and submitted online by close of business on 2nd May 2025.

Dated at Accra this 3rd day of April 2025
By order of the Board
Company Secretary

Registration Information

All shareholders interested in attending the NSIA 46th Annual General Meeting must complete the registration process.

1

Visit the official Zoom registration portal at https://us02web.zoom.us/meeting/register/YRkseKZzTUyGqZdGD4SaNQ

2

Complete the registration form with accurate contact information

3

Submit your registration and await confirmation email with unique Zoom access credentials

Note: Upon successful registration, you will receive access to the audited financial statements and the CV of Mrs. Monica Amissah.

Proxy Appointment

Shareholders who cannot attend the AGM may appoint a proxy to attend and vote on their behalf. A proxy need not be a shareholder of the Company.

Proxy Submission Guidelines

  1. Download the proxy form available below
  2. Complete all required fields with accurate information
  3. Submit the completed form via email to: akuaa@oxfordconsultantsgh.com
  4. Ensure your submission is received no later than close of business on 2nd May 2025

Upon successful submission, both the shareholder and appointed proxy will receive confirmation emails with relevant details.
